### GMCF
- Definition：Global Muslim Charitable Foundation — the charity whose multi-page portal this repository implements. The package name `gmcf-charity-portal` and the README title identify it as the organization behind the site.
- Aliases：Global Muslim Charitable Foundation

### Zakat Calculator
- Definition：A 10-field Shariah-compliant assessment tool on the Zakat Fund page that computes gold/silver benchmarks, applies a Nisab qualifier, shows live totals, supports reset and a fulfillment flow, and can apply its result to a donation.
- Aliases：zakat-calculator、Quick Zakat

### Appeals & Emergency Causes
- Definition：The appeals listing page (/appeals) where donors browse causes with category pills, live search, sorting, and a quick-donate modal with preset chips.
- Aliases：appeals

### Impact Case Dossiers
- Definition：The Impact page's case-study entries, each with modals, filters, and direct sponsorship tiers.
- Aliases：impact dossiers、case studies

### One Meal One Hope
- Definition：The featured campaign spotlight on /campaign-details, including tabbed story/budget/dispatch/governance panels, a photo gallery switcher, meal-package selection, one-time vs monthly giving, and a live meal counter.
- Aliases：campaign details

### Volunteer Portal
- Definition：The youth volunteers field portal at /volunteer offering pathway preselection, an application form with success confirmation, and an FAQ accordion.
- Aliases：volunteer
