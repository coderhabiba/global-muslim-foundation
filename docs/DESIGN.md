## Brand & Style

This design system expresses transparent humanitarian stewardship, serene dignity, and urgent community impact. It serves donors, volunteers, institutional partners, and crisis beneficiaries seeking immediate clarity, ethical integrity, and spiritual resonance. The emotional experience balances quiet reverence with decisive calls to direct action.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Clean Editorial** with **Soft Tactile Humanism**:
- Pure, airy canvases framed by deep evergreen and teal foundations that signify longevity and institutional stability.
- Searing, energetic amber and honey-gold highlights to elevate emergency relief, impact milestones, and zakat/sadaqah donation flows.
- Nuanced micro-details including hairline gold accents, 8-point geometric badge framing, and generous breathing room that replace traditional ornate clutter with focused, contemporary trust.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in natural marine teals and deep forest greens, establishing ethical accountability and environmental stewardship. Radiant warm ambers represent hope, immediate relief, and active giving.

### Application Rules
- **Primary (`#0D7C66`)**: Institutional identity, key navigational elements, active progress meters, and primary headers.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: High-priority conversion moments—specifically the "Donate Now", "Give Zakat", and urgent relief appeal triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#044D44`)**: Deepest anchor shade for high-contrast footer blocks, trust certifications, and authoritative financial transparency summaries.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937`)**: Primary slate text color providing stark, accessible readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAF9`)**: Main content containers and section alternating backgrounds, keeping cognitive load minimal.
- **Subtle Mint Accent (`#EEF8F2`)**: Applied behind campaign metric cards, badges, and verified distribution tallies.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages **Inter** for bilingual English-Bangla environments (complementing standard script fallbacks like Hind Siliguri and Anek Bangla). Inter offers tall x-heights, distinct letterforms, and uniform weights across varied language scripts.

### Hierarchy & Rhythm
- Headlines maintain tight negative letter-spacing to emphasize authority and journalistic gravity on emergency campaigns.
- Body text retains relaxed line heights (1.5x to 1.55x) to guarantee comfort when reading long field reports, financial audits, and project diaries.
- Labels and interactive cues utilize medium to semi-bold weights with subtle tracking to maximize scan speed in forms and checkout summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid anchors the layout on desktop (max width 1280px), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile devices.

### Viewport Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Outer margins set to `1rem`, columns stack vertically, donation quick-pick amounts shift into a horizontal scrolling row or 2-column grid.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: Outer margins set to `2rem`, gutters at `1rem`, enabling side-by-side metric badges and dual-column appeal layouts.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: Content centers within a maximum width of `1280px` with `4rem` outer margin, providing generous breathing space between campaign narratives and sticky donation calculators.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of calm, dimensional stability achieved through tonal surfaces, fine outlines, and soft tinted ambient occlusions.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Pure `#F8FAF9` page base and transparent card containers.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Relief)**: Content cards and appeal containers sit on `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px border (`#E5E7EB`) and a feather-soft ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(4, 77, 68, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating)**: Hovered appeal cards and focused form modals: `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(4, 77, 68, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Urgent Overlays)**: Emergency banners, donation confirmation dialogs, and drawer navigation use deep teal backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(0, 74, 80, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances organic, compassionate warmth with architectural stability:
- Cards, modular panels, and media containers utilize a standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px), scaling up to `1rem` (16px) for featured hero blocks and giving modules.
- Form inputs, amount selectors, and primary buttons use consistent `0.5rem` rounding to preserve crisp touch targets.
- Badges, status chips, and emergency appeal indicators adopt pill geometry (`9999px`) for quick scanning.
- Subtle Islamic architectural flourishes are restricted to badge corners, octagonal framing icons, and clean geometric divider lines rather than ornate background graphics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Emergency CTA**: Solid vibrant amber (`#F59E0B`) with dark slate (`#1F2937`) text, semi-bold weight, transitioning to `#D97706` on hover. Used for "Donate", "Give Zakat", and emergency actions.
- **Secondary / Institutional**: Solid deep emerald (`#0D7C66`) with pure white text, transitioning to `#044D44` on hover. Used for "Learn More", "View Reports", and account functions.
- **Ghost / Outlined**: Transparent background with 1.5px border in `#0D7C66`, text in `#0D7C66`.

### Donation Amount Selector Chips
- Selectable pill-style buttons displaying set denominations ($25, $50, $100, custom).
- Default: Border 1px `#E5E7EB`, surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#374151`.
- Active/Selected: Surface `#EEF8F2`, border 2px `#0D7C66`, text `#044D44` with a checkmark badge.

### Campaign & Cause Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a 1px `#E5E7EB` border and `rounded-lg` geometry.
- Aspect ratio 16:9 media preview with a floating category tag in the upper-left (e.g., "Gaza Emergency", "Clean Water").
- Includes dual metric progress bars: Teal fill (`#0D7C66`) on light green background (`#EEF8F2`) displaying percentage raised, target goal, and donor count.

### Input Fields & Calculation Forms
- Height of 48px to accommodate responsive mobile taps.
- Inactive: Border 1px `#D1D5DB`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1F2937`, label floating or anchored at `label-md`.
- Focus: Border 2px `#0D7C66`, ambient glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 124, 102, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 20x20px with 4px corner rounding on checkboxes, full circles for radios.
- Inactive state uses `#D1D5DB` borders. Checked state fills with `#0D7C66` and white icon glyphs.

### Zakat & Impact Metrics Ribbon
- High-contrast banner component resting on `#044D44` background with `#FEF3C7` and white text tokens.
- Houses live transparency tallies (Families Fed, Wells Constructed, Medical Packs Delivered) punctuated by thin geometric gold star dividers (`#F59E0B`).
