CASE DOSSIER #BD-109 Verified Reintegration

Case Investigation: Badshah’s Safe Homecoming

Initial Encounter: Child discovered on platform 3, Tejgaon Station, showing signs of severe trauma, dehydration, and acute distress.

Emergency Shelter: Transferred to GMCF Child Protection Transit Center in Mirpur. Provided nutritional stabilization and daily pediatric checkups.

Tracing Procedure: Social caseworkers deployed geo-targeted photo notices across 12 Upazilas over 18 days with district police liaison.

Family Confirmation: Maternal match confirmed via biometric birth certificates and formal Union Parishad affidavit.

Audited by: Sheikh Maulana Tariq • Chief Shariah Supervisor

Saifan’s Pediatric Clinical Ledger

Full cost breakdown covered via 100% Zakat fund: Emergency pediatric triage ($45), Medication & post-operative rehabilitation ($85), 6 months growth nutrition packs ($50). Direct hospital receipts verified by Kurigram District Hospital.
