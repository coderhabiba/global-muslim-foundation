One Meal One Hope — Nourishing Vulnerable Lives Across Railway Communities

Partnered with Pally Life Development Society (PLDS) and local community kitchens to provide fresh hot cooked meals, bottled clean water, and fruit juice packs to malnourished children living along railway tracks in Tejgaon, Kamalapur, and Mirpur.

No Child Should Sleep with an Empty Stomach on the Cold Concrete

Every evening at the Tejgaon and Kamalapur railway stations in Dhaka, hundreds of children gather not to take a train, but to find shelter from the night. Displaced by riverbank erosion, acute rural poverty, and domestic tragedies, these youngsters scavenge for survival. Most subsist on discarded scrap food or go without a warm meal for over 36 consecutive hours.

Through the One Meal One Hope initiative, our mission is to eliminate acute hunger along the rail corridor. Each single serving is not merely calories—it is freshly prepared high-protein nutrition, purified safe drinking water, and unconditional human dignity served with warmth and respect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity on donations, ground execution, and tax receipts.

Can I specify my donation strictly as Zakat vs General Sadaqah? expand_more

Yes. During giving, selecting the "Designate as Zakat" checkbox routes your contribution into our ring-fenced Zakat bank account. It is exclusively deployed to purchase direct meals given directly to eligible impoverished individuals without operational deductions.

Can overseas donors visit the community kitchens in Dhaka? expand_more

Absolutely. We maintain an open-door policy for all donors and independent observers. Contact our Gulshan-2 HQ at least 48 hours prior, and our donor-relations officers will arrange a guided field briefing and cooking inspection at the Tejgaon operations kitchen.

Will I receive an official tax-deductible donation receipt? expand_more

Yes. Instantly after checkout, an automated receipt with UK Gift Aid confirmation (Charity Reg #1184930) or US 501(c)(3) tax exemption identification is sent to your registered email address.
